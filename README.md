# OrcTo60

A small WoW Forever addon with two solo 1–60 routes for a fresh Horde Orc. It picks **Beast Mastery Hunter** or **Combat Rogue** from your character class. Both checklists assume a Normal realm and character-earned gold and gear, with no auction house, boosts, alt funding, or Legacy bonuses.

## Install and use

1. Copy the `OrcTo60` folder to the Forever client's `Interface\AddOns` directory. The resulting path should end in `Interface\AddOns\OrcTo60\OrcTo60.toc`.
2. Enable **OrcTo60** at character select and log in on a Horde Orc Hunter or Rogue. Type `/orc60` to show or hide the window. Drag its background to move it.
3. Follow the active step. Quest pickups, objectives, and turn-ins advance as the quest log updates. Use **Next** for travel and training, or to pass a step you chose to skip. **Back** reviews earlier steps and **Current step** returns to your saved position.
4. Optional. Install the Forever build of TomTom and click **Waypoint** on a step that shows coordinates. It sends one point at a time. With the default WoW map, use the coordinates and quest markers. [TomTom for Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/tomtom/files/9426121)

The clock starts at the first login with the addon enabled and includes logged-out time. It stops when the character reaches 60. Starting above level 1 or with XP already earned begins at the closest earlier route chapter and marks the timer as a partial run. Each character has its own saved position and clock.

## Combat Rogue

- **Quest circuits.** Pick up the listed quests before leaving the hub. Do all shared objectives on the same pass, then hand in at the hub or along your exit route. Follow-ups appear after their prerequisites. When upgrading from 0.2.0, an existing Rogue resumes at the start of the same chapter. The addon checks completed quests automatically as you work through it.
- **Two-hour sessions.** Start at the next active step. Stack kills and pickups into one circuit and use the last 10–15 minutes to hand in, repair, buy food and poison supplies, and log out in a rested area if it is on the way. **Session stop** steps mark convenient places to pause. Press **Next** when continuing. Compare circuits using XP gained and `/played` from pickup through turn-in, including waits and recovery.
- **Talents.** The panel shows each point through 60. The build is 31 Combat and 20 Assassination, with Riposte at 21, Blade Flurry at 30, and Adrenaline Rush at 40. Train Vanish at 22 and Shank at 24. Start with single pulls and spend Blade Flurry on clean pairs that advance quests together. [Forever Rogue talents](https://metabot.gg/en/wow-forever/talent-calculator/rogue) · [Trainer abilities](https://metabot.gg/en/wow-forever/classes/rogue)
- **Poisons and travel.** Practice lockpicking to 75 during the Ratchet quest. Do Fizzule's tower after 20, then finish the poison unlock at Tarren Mill before starting its quest circuits. Save Sprint for gaps between camps and set aside gold for riding at 40. [Horde poison quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=2478/mission-possible-but-not-probable) · [Hinott's Assistance](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=2480/hinotts-assistance)
- **Weapons.** Start with a vendor Stiletto or a good drop. Take Pointed Axe from Centaur Bracers and Blade of Cunning from the Ratchet chain. Learn swords from Archibald on the Undercity stop. Longsword at 21, Broadsword at 31, and Falchion at 41 are vendor fallbacks. Look for Zorbin's Mega-Slicer in Feralas, Intrepid Shortsword from the Plaguelands cauldrons, and Claw of Shalla'kal from Kits and Cats in Hyjal. The panel shows the recommendation for your current level. [Orc Rogue weapon skills](https://foreverwow.com/weapons/rogue/) · [Forever vendor swords](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/items/weapons/one-handed-swords/quality:1) · [Zapped Giants](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=7003/zapped-giants) · [Mission Accomplished!](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=5238/mission-accomplished) · [Kits and Cats](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93615/kits-and-cats)
- **Optional weapon detours.** Use the outdoor Baron Aquanis quest for Outlaw Sabre if it is available during Ashenvale. After *The Horn of Xelthos*, check for C's follow-up letter. *The Eye of Bhossca* is a solo disguise and lockpicking job in Scarlet Monastery with Ruby-Adorned Blade as its payoff. Treat it as a dedicated session when you are already near Undercity. At Gadgetzan, check Rettrick's dagger if you reach Honored through the route. [Outlaw Sabre](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=16886/outlaw-sabre) · [The Eye of Bhossca](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=78676/the-eye-of-bhossca) · [Gadgetzan rewards](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/faction=369/gadgetzan)

## Beast Mastery Hunter

- Keep pet food and ammo stocked. Send the pet before firing, avoid clipping Auto Shot while moving, and use traps and Feign Death to protect the time you have already put into a pull. Use Summon Hawk for targets that will live through it. It shares a cooldown with Arcane Shot. [Forever Hunter abilities and talents](https://metabot.gg/en/wow-forever/classes/hunter)
- Hunter is the route's speed pick because pet threat supports sustained pulls and Cheetah cuts movement early. Affliction Warlock is a close alternative with self-sustain and the level-40 Felsteed. Frost Mage's larger pulls depend more on suitable camps and clean resets. The checklist spends 33 Beast Mastery and 18 Marksmanship points through 60. [Hunter talent tree](https://metabot.gg/en/wow-forever/talent-calculator/hunter) · [Blizzard hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-25-2026)
- Take the first ranged upgrade on route, then check vendor bows around 10 and 20. Master Hunter's Bow is an optional Stranglethorn payoff if your bow is behind and the named targets are up. Truthseeker's Bow is worth a turn-in from level 40 if you already collected 25 library books in passing. Prioritize level-40 riding over shopping for small upgrades. [Heavy Recurve Bow](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3027/heavy-recurve-bow) · [Big Game Hunter](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=208/big-game-hunter) · [Truthseeker's Bow](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=277254/truthseekers-bow) · [Riding and mount locations](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/unlocking-riding-skill-mounts)
- Level gates give nearby quests or camps for extra XP. Finish easy quest overlaps before grinding. If a named target is contested or a dangerous pull fails, use the local fallback. The later gates can require substantial additional mob XP depending on how many side quests and kills you pick up.

## Route data and verification

Edit `OrcTo60/Route.lua` for Hunter and `OrcTo60/Rogue.lua` for Rogue. `OrcTo60/Quests.lua` holds the shared quest IDs, pickup and turn-in names, and map points. The addon uses interface `16001` and works without other addons.

The Hunter steps and talent order matched the previous version in an offline comparison. Lua 5.1 checks covered the Rogue quest order, 51 talent points, weapon advice for all 60 levels, saved positions, the wall clock, both class selections, and TomTom. The late level gates can still require additional quest or kill XP. The two-hour stop markers are pacing suggestions. In-game testing and timed 1–60 runs are pending.
